### Story flow

1. **The Key in the Box** — reach the first location and guide the key through the tilt maze.
2. **The Hidden Repair Card** — reach the second location and scan the creator's printable QR repair card. The backup code remains available when scanning is not possible.
3. **Fill the Meeting Lamp** — reach the final location and tilt the phone carefully to pour the lamp oil without spilling it.
